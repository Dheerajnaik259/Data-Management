# SMM Ops Tool - Future Feature Roadmap & Scope

This document outlines high-value operational enhancements tailored for scaling Social Media Management (SMM) agencies and video production teams.

---

## High-Impact Feature Enhancements

### 1. Monthly Retainer & Content Quota Tracker
- **Overview**: Track monthly deliverable quotas per client retainer contract (e.g. *12 Reels + 4 Stories per month*).
- **Key Features**:
  - Quota progress bar on Client Profile (e.g. `Client X: 8 / 12 Reels Completed in August`).
  - Automatic warning badges when a client is behind on promised monthly output.
- **Value**: Prevents client churn by ensuring agency deliverable commitments are fulfilled on time every month.

---

### 2. Deliverable Production Pipeline (Raw → Edit → Published)
- **Overview**: Move beyond simple cloud storage links by tracking the post-production stage of every asset.
- **Pipeline Stages**:
  - `Raw Footage Uploaded` → `In Post-Production / Editing` → `Client Review` → `Approved & Published`
- **Key Features**:
  - Visual status pill tags for each deliverable item.
  - Editor assignment and target delivery deadlines.
- **Value**: Eliminates client WhatsApp pings asking *"Where is the video edit?"*.

---

### 3. Daily Crew Dispatch Desk (1-Click Call Sheets)
- **Overview**: A centralized call-sheet view for scheduled crew members across all shoots on a given day.
- **Key Features**:
  - Filter shoots by *Today* or *Tomorrow*.
  - Prefilled 1-click WhatsApp schedule notifications to all assigned cameramen.
  - Quick check-in toggle and status confirmation.
- **Value**: Saves 15–20 minutes every morning during heavy production weeks.

---

### 4. Client Profitability & Margin Analytics
- **Overview**: Financial intelligence dashboard evaluating true client profitability after crew and travel costs.
- **Key Metrics**:
  - Gross Revenue vs Total Production Expenses per Client.
  - Net Profit Margin (%) and Margin Ranking.
- **Value**: Empowers agency founders to identify top-performing, high-margin accounts vs high-maintenance, low-margin clients.

---

### 5. Contract Expiry & Retainer Renewal Reminders
- **Overview**: Automated tracking for retainer agreement end-dates (3, 6, 12 months).
- **Key Features**:
  - Contract Renewal Date field on Client records.
  - Banner notifications 14 days before contract expiration.
- **Value**: Ensures seamless retainer renewals without billing gaps or uncontracted work.
